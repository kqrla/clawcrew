# positioning

the nine w's, applied to the factory itself. each line names its evidence. where there is no
evidence yet, the line says so, because a positioning document that reads as complete on day one is
a positioning document that was invented.

| dimension | this project | evidence | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **who** | operators with a SaaS idea and no team, who would otherwise hire | `docs/00` intent; the user's own words across sessions | user-stated |
| **what** | a long-running org that takes a business from phase gates to a shipped product, with an audit trail a non-AI third party can read | `docs/30`, `docs/33`, 423 tests | verified |
| **when** | now, because agent runtime got cheap enough that the binding constraint moved from compute to oversight | cost model in `test-projects/meenible/brief.md` | partial |
| **where** | TypeScript + Postgres 16 on InsForge, GitHub for source, provider APIs for money and fulfilment | `toolbox/stack.md` | verified |
| **why** | a founder's month is spent on coordination, not craft. an org that coordinates itself returns that month | no external evidence yet | gap |
| **why-not** | it cannot invent demand, it cannot license a character, and it cannot make a print house faster. every one of those is a human's job | `directive/refusals.md` | user-stated |
| **world** | other agent frameworks orchestrate tools. this one is judged by whether a business survives a month unattended | `docs/30` gates | partial |
| **way** | month one is one business, meenible, with ten slices; the second business is a config change, not a rewrite | `scope-management/readme.md` | plan |
| **wire** | the audit trail and the heartbeat are the message layer: findings post where humans already look | `docs/33` | plan |

## the gaps that matter

**why** is the one with no evidence at all, and it is the one that decides the project. the claim
"coordination eats the founder's month" is a belief until it is measured against somebody's actual
calendar. it is recorded as a gap rather than softened into a partial.

**wire** is a plan. there is no human-facing surface yet, so the audit trail is legible in principle
and unread in practice. that is the honest state and it is the next thing after the lifecycle verbs.

## what this is not

not a pitch. nothing here is written to convince a reader who has not already decided. the reader is
the org itself, at 3am, deciding whether a thing it wants to do is allowed.
